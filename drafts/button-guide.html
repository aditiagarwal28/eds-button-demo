Choosing the Right Button

Every click is either a trip to another page or an action on this one. Pick your button accordingly.

Use the OOTB Button

A formatted link, no block needed. Available to every author in the rich text editor.

  • The click should navigate to another page, anchor, or file
  • No client-side logic or state is required
  • Speed of authoring matters more than customization

Examples: "View Size Guide", "Read Reviews", "Download Spec Sheet"

Use a Custom Button Block

A dedicated block with its own model and JavaScript. Built once by developers, reused by every author.

  • The click should trigger behavior on the current page
  • It needs state: loading, success, disabled
  • It calls an API, fires analytics, or updates the DOM

Examples: "Add to Cart", "Copy Share Link", "Open Filters"
